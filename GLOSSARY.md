# kosha

A personal finance app for recording and making sense of one's own money.

## Glossary

**Ledger**
The area of the app for recording and organising money. Categories and tags
live here, and transactions will join them.

**Category**
A user's own label for what kind of money something is, made of a name, an
icon and a colour (e.g. Groceries, Rent, Salary). A transaction will have
exactly one category. Names are unique per user, ignoring case. Every user has
their own categories; none are shared or seeded.

**Description** (of a category)
An optional short line saying what belongs in a category, e.g. "Supermarket
and vegetable vendor, not eating out". Shown wherever categories are listed.

**Note** (of a category)
Longer, optional free text the user keeps about a category, such as reminders
or budget thoughts. Seen only when editing the category.

**Tag**
A user's own label, just a name, that cuts across categories (e.g.
goa-trip-2026, reimbursable, gift). A transaction will have any number of tags.
Names are unique per user, ignoring case. Every user has their own tags; none
are shared or seeded.
