# kosha

A personal finance app for recording and making sense of one's own money.

## Glossary

**Ledger**
The area of the app for recording and organising money. Categories live here,
and transactions will join them.

**Category**
A user's own label for grouping money, made of a name, an icon and a colour
(e.g. Groceries, Rent, Salary). Names are unique per user, ignoring case.
Every user has their own categories; none are shared or seeded.
